# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give specific, actionable review feedback.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and credit contributions.
- Respect privacy, security disclosures, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publication of private information without permission are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and without retaliation.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community events, and public representation of the project.
